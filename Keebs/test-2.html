AULA F75 Wireless Mechanical Keyboard Review: The Budget King

Finding a mechanical keyboard that bridges the gap between enthusiast-grade acoustics and budget-friendly pricing is rare. The AULA F75 Wireless Mechanical Keyboard achieves just that, packing custom-level features into a readily accessible pre-built package.

Design and Build Quality

The keyboard features an optimized 75% compact layout, saving valuable desk space without sacrificing essential navigation and function keys. Built around a leaf-spring gasket structure, it offers a flex-friendly design that reduces typing fatigue. Furthermore, its impressive five-layer internal sound-dampening construction—featuring Poron foam, an IXPE switch pad, and bottom silicone—eliminates hollow resonances to deliver a clean, satisfying acoustic profile.

Performance and Features

Under the hood, the F75 comes equipped with factory-lubricated LEOBOG Reaper switches, providing butter-smooth linear keystrokes straight out of the box. Keycaps are crafted from durable double-shot PBT in a comfortable Cherry profile. Connectivity is entirely versatile, offering three-way options: ultra-responsive 2.4GHz wireless, Bluetooth, and wired USB-C, powered by a robust 4000mAh battery that guarantees long sessions between charges.
